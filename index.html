Provenance & Mastery

Crafted With Intention. Worn With Confidence.

Every piece in the Josie Footies archive is sculpted by hand between our master shoemaking roots in Ibadan and our styling studios in Gbagada, Lagos. We honor the deep tradition of West African leather finishing while engineering European minimalist architectural silhouettes.
